PACK & TEO
A Nuclear Survival Story

The bombs fell three weeks ago. Most didn't make it. You did — barely. They call you Pack, on account of the massive backpack you refuse to put down. Everything you own is strapped to your back.

Well, everything and everyone. A tiny hamster named Teo rides in a pouch on your pack. He's the last friend you have in this broken world.

By day, scavengers hunt for supplies — and they'll kill for yours. By night, radiation-mutated creatures crawl from the ruins. You need to find shelter, build defenses, and survive.

Whatever happens... keep Teo safe.
